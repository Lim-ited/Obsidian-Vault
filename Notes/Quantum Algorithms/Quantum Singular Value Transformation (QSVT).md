**Quantum singular value transformation** is a framework for designing [quantum algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_algorithm "Quantum algorithm"). It encompasses a variety of quantum algorithms for problems that can be solved with [linear algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_algebra "Linear algebra"), including [Hamiltonian simulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_simulation "Hamiltonian simulation"), [search problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grover's_algorithm "Grover's algorithm"), and [linear system solving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HHL_algorithm).[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_singular_value_transformation#cite_note-qsvt2019-1)[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_singular_value_transformation#cite_note-grand_unification2021-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_singular_value_transformation#cite_note-3) It was introduced in 2018 by András Gilyén, Yuan Su, Guang Hao Low, and Nathan Wiebe, generalizing algorithms for Hamiltonian simulation of Guang Hao Low and [Isaac Chuang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Chuang "Isaac Chuang") inspired by signal processing.
